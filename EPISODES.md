## Topic queue
Take the first item that is not in the register. Every premise must be buildable from Blender primitives, safe to show, and its science checkable. Verify every number before use; the notes are starting points, not facts.

1. ~~**Diving into a pool of mercury**~~ (done, TIL-02): density 13.5, you float with ~7 % submerged, can't dive or swim; the danger is the vapour above the pool.
2. **A ship launched into a sea of honey**: floats ~28 % higher, honey ~10,000× more viscous than water, the launch wave never travels.
3. **Gravity reversed for one second**: rise 4.9 m in the second, peak ~10 m, fall back at ~14 m/s; indoors is safer.
4. **Friction switched off for one minute**: you can stand but not walk; knots slip, nails lose grip; friction returns at full speed.
5. **Shrunk to 1 cm tall**: falls are harmless, a raindrop is a trap, you'd have to eat like a shrew.
6. **Running a marathon at the speed of sound**: about 2 minutes; ~35 kN of drag; the tightest corner has a ~12 km radius.
7. **Shouting on Venus**: 92 bar, ~465 °C, air ~65× denser; your voice changes (hedge: modelled, uncertain).
8. **Every cat on Earth meowing at once**: total sound power less than one car engine; sound arrives staggered.
9. **Earth stops spinning for one second**: everything loose keeps moving east at up to ~465 m/s; at ~45° N that is about the speed of sound.
10. **Boiling pasta on Mars**: water boils near 0 °C at Mars pressure; the pasta never cooks; the fix is a pressure cooker.
11. **A bowling ball dropped onto a trampoline made of soap film** (pick only if the science checks out cleanly).
12. **A cup of coffee the size of a lake**: how long it would take to cool, and what the steam would do.
